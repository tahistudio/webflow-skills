# Nudges and difficult conversations

All drafts. Nothing is sent without a yes. Follow `tahi:tahi-brand-voice` when it is installed. Escalate to Liam before replying on money, refunds, the contract, a client threatening to leave, or anything legal.

## Waiting on the client

Timings come from `tahi-client:client-kickoff` › references/working-agreement.md.

**Reminder** (when the turnaround passes; often the dashboard's own nudge, or `send_nudge` after a yes):

> Hey [Name], a quick reminder that we're waiting on [the Home page copy] to keep [design] moving. Could you send it by [date]?

**Personal message** (two working days later, from the Tahi lead):

> Hey [Name],
>
> We still need [the Home page copy] to keep things moving. Without it, [design can't be finished / we'd launch on date instead of date].
>
> If it's tricky to get, two options: we can [use the current site's copy as a starting point / build with placeholder text and swap it in later], or we can move the dates now so nobody's rushed. Which would suit?
>
> Cheers,
> [Name]

**Re-dating** (10 working days):

> Hey [Name],
>
> Because [the copy] hasn't come through yet, we've moved the plan: [design approval is now date, launch is now date]. Once it arrives, we'll pick up straight away.
>
> If those dates don't work, let's talk about what can come out of the first launch to hold the date.

**Pause** (20 working days):

> Hey [Name],
>
> We haven't been able to move [project] forward for about four weeks while we wait on [item]. We've paused it on our side so it's not sitting half-finished in our schedule.
>
> Nothing is lost. When you're ready, reply here and we'll agree a new start date together. [Anything about invoicing for work done to date: confirm with Liam before sending.]

## Tahi is running late

Same working day you know. Own it, give the new date, say what changes so it does not happen again.

> Hey [Name],
>
> The [build of the product pages] is taking longer than we planned. [One plain reason: the integration needed more work than expected / we underestimated the number of layouts]. That's on us.
>
> The new date is [date]. To protect the launch, we're [what you are doing]. Launch stays on [date] / moves to [date].
>
> I'll update you on [day] either way.

Standard and full: call first if the launch date moves, then send this as the written summary.

## The client is running late

Use the waiting-on-the-client sequence above. If the delay comes from their side but they ask Tahi to hold the original date, give the options plainly:

> We can still launch on [date] if we [launch with fewer pages / use your existing copy for now / move the blog to a second release]. Keeping everything in and keeping the date isn't possible without rushing the testing, which we won't do.

## Design disagreement

Separate taste from goals. Taste is theirs; goals, evidence and the quality floor are where Tahi earns its view.

> I can see why [the darker version] appeals. Our concern is [goal-based reason]. Two ways forward: we go with your version, or we test both with [three people from your audience / your sales team] and pick the one that works. Either is fine with us; it's your brand.

If they choose their version, do it well, record it in `docs/decisions.md`, and do not reopen it.

## A new stakeholder reopens approved work

> Welcome to the project, [Name]. So you have the background: [design] was approved by [approver] on [date], and we're now building it. We're glad to hear your thoughts. Anything that changes approved work becomes a change with its own cost and timing, and [approver] would need to agree it. Could you and [approver] send us one combined view by [date]?

## The client is unhappy with quality

Listen first, ask for specifics, fix what is ours quickly, and do not argue in writing.

> Thanks for telling us straight. I'd rather hear it now. So we fix the right things, could you point to [the pages or parts] that feel wrong, and what you expected instead? I'll come back by [date] with what we'll change and when.

Then: triage as feedback (SKILL.md section 2). Anything below the definition of done is a fix at Tahi's cost. Anything beyond it is a change. Tell Liam the same day.

## Budget or hours running out

Raise it at about 75 percent of the budget used, not at 100.

> We've used about three quarters of the budget, with [what is left] still to do. We're on track / We think the remaining work needs about [n] more hours than planned, because [reason]. Options: [trim the scope: item], or [add budget: confirm with Liam]. Which do you prefer?
