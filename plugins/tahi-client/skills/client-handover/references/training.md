# Training: live session and short video

Train on the real site with real content. Every task taught is a task in the editor guide; nothing is taught that is not written down.

## Live session agenda (60 minutes)

Standard: one session. Full: one per editor group, trimmed to that group's tasks. Ask everyone's agreement before recording.

| Time | Item |
|---|---|
| 5 min | What we'll cover, and where the guide and recording will live. |
| 5 min | Tour: signing in, finding their way around, what is theirs to change and what is not. |
| 25 min | **They drive.** Each editor does the top three tasks from the guide on the live site or staging, with real content, while we watch. Tahi does not take the mouse. |
| 10 min | Mistakes and recovery: undo, restore an earlier version, unpublish, who to tell. |
| 5 min | Publishing and checking the result on a phone. |
| 10 min | Questions, then the support window, how to raise a request, the reply time, and the monthly plan if one is agreed. |

**After the session,** the same day: send the recording, the guide, and a list of any questions that need a written answer. Fix any guide step that confused someone in the room.

## Short training video (5 to 8 minutes)

Every handover gets one, light process included. One take is fine; edit only to cut dead air. Screen recording with voice, at a readable zoom.

**Script**

1. **Open (20 seconds).** "This is a quick guide to updating [site name]. In about [n] minutes you'll see how to [task one], [task two] and [task three]. The written guide is linked below."
2. **Signing in (30 seconds).** Where to go, which account, where the site is.
3. **Task one (1 to 2 minutes).** The most common job. Do it start to finish at a normal pace, naming each click. End by showing the change live.
4. **Task two (1 to 2 minutes).** The second most common job.
5. **Task three (1 minute).** Only if it is genuinely common.
6. **Don't touch (30 seconds).** The two or three things to leave alone, and why.
7. **When it goes wrong (30 seconds).** How to undo, and how to raise a request with us.
8. **Close (10 seconds).** "That's it. The written guide has every step. If anything's unclear, raise a request and we'll help."

Write the voice-over in the client's words, not Webflow's or Sanity's: "the blog list", not "the Blog Posts collection template". Follow `tahi:tahi-brand-voice` when it is installed.

Link the video in the handover pack and the dashboard handover request.
